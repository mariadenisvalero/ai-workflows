# Sustainability claims — vetted language only

Source: TAG's RL Copy Master Guide (Sustainability tab), tagline **"Design the Change."** This is compliance-sensitive: the FTC and jurisdictions like California actively regulate environmental marketing claims, and RL can be fined for improper use. **Do not write your own sustainability claim — use only the vetted language below, and do not alter it**, not even a preposition swap ("through" vs. "with" can change the legal meaning).

## Never use these words/phrases (vague, unregulated, or actively risky)
- "Better for the Earth"
- "Better for the Environment"
- "Biodegradable" — California has especially strict rules here; loop in legal before ever using it
- "Eco-Friendly"
- "Environmentally-Friendly"
- "Green"
- "Leave the earth better than we found it"

Instead: be specific, reference an actual certification, and use the approved consumer-facing language below.

## Process claims (not material-specific)
- **Water use** (goal: 20%+ reduction across operations/value chain by 2025) — Ecofast: "Dyed with Ecofast, using processes that reduce water use during manufacturing." Avitera® SE Dyestuff: same pattern, swap the certification name.
- **Chemical use / Considered Chemistry** (goal: eliminate hazardous chemicals in supply chain by 2025) — Bluesign®: "This fabric is certified as bluesign® approved." The OEKO-TEX family (Standard 100, Eco Passport, STeP, Leather Standard, Made in Green) and EU Ecolabel: "Oeko-Tex certified" (no separate claim per sub-certification beyond that, except Made in Green: "Made using processes that minimize the impacts of production on the environment and your health"). GreenScreen Certified™ has no approved standalone consumer claim.
- **Animal welfare** (craftsmanship/fair-trade communities) — Nest Seal of Ethical Handcraft; Fairtrade / Fair for Life / World Fair Trade Organisation / Fair Trade Certified. Only use when an actual certification/verification exists for that specific product — never as a general vibe.
- **Cross-cutting**: Cradle to Cradle Certified (product-level) — promotes considered community, reduced water use, reduced waste, and considered chemistry all at once: "Cradle to Cradle Certified."

## Quick definitions (for internal use, not necessarily consumer copy)
- **Carbon footprint** — greenhouse-gas emissions (carbon equivalent) for an activity/organization over time.
- **Pre-consumer waste** — manufacturing scrap reintroduced into production (trimmings, defective units) before it ever reached a consumer.
- **Post-consumer waste** — waste generated by the end consumer after the product's intended use.
- **Value chain** — the full set of activities a business performs to deliver value, broader than "supply chain."

If a certification or claim isn't listed here, don't improvise — check with the user for the current vetted wording (the source guide names a sustainability contact for this at Ralph Lauren; ask the user if a fresh confirmation is needed).
