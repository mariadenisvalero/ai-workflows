# Lauren — brand voice

For typography, color hex codes, type scale, padding, and fixed-module visual specs, see the Figma assembly skill instead — this file is copy/story only.

## Color palette — fixed, not derived from photos
Unlike other brands (see `color-palette.md`'s general rule of deriving 2–3 colors from the campaign photography), **Lauren emails always use the same fixed palette**, most visibly on the logo/header module:
- Background: `#F7F1EB` (warm cream)
- Logo & text: `#1D1D1D` (near-black)

Use this pair by default for Lauren's non-photo blocks rather than sampling a color from that campaign's images. If a specific brief explicitly asks for a different treatment, flag that as a deliberate deviation in the Email Plan's NOTES rather than silently overriding the brand default.

## Overview
Introduced 1996. Leading brand for naturally confident women, feminine and timeless silhouettes at a more accessible price than the luxury sub-brands. Key themes: natural beauty, effortless elegance, simple moments. Working tagline: **"Forever Lauren."** Lauren inherits the Master Brand pillars (`master-brand.md`) expressed through a more accessible, democratic register than Collection/Purple Label.

## The story
"Weekdays in the city. Saturdays at the flower market. A stroll beside the sea with those we love." The Lauren woman is a portrait of timeless sophistication — beauty found in the everyday. One of RL's most democratic, approachable, female-focused brands.

## Tone pillars
- **CONFIDENT** — true to herself, inspires others with her self-belief; never egotistical. Elevated but never didactic vocabulary. **Active voice only.**
- **CHARMING** — delightful, playful storytelling — but never whimsical, poetic, or winky.
- **ILLUSTRATIVE** — occasional romantic flourish to set a mood; product descriptions "deserve a little love," but stay balanced, never overwrought.
- **SOPHISTICATED** — clear and direct; no room for misinterpretation; effortless in its presentation.
- **EMPOWERING** — warm, welcoming, aspirational-yet-obtainable; cheers on other women; optimistic, friendly, inviting.
- **GRACEFUL** — polished but not affected; grace from effortless ease, not from putting on airs.

**Approved example (product description)**: *"This utilitarian-inspired patch-pocket shirt is crafted from soft, lightweight cotton and features a boldly graphic animal print."*

**Avoid** (flagged as tonally incorrect in the guide): generic "we hope these bring you comfort" filler, unvetted sustainability claims ("grown with fresh air and sunshine — no harmful herbicides"; see `sustainability-claims.md` for what's actually approved), empty enthusiasm ("Happy colors. Beautiful prints... because we all need a little happy"), and cute-but-vague hooks that don't earn their place ("stunning new styles and fresh ways to wear them").

## Story best practices
Consistent look and feel across a campaign; avoid repeating identical image crops within one email; mix full-outfit and close-crop shots; use laydowns where the image mapping offers them.

## Known fixed-shape modules for this brand
- **Sizes Modules** — dedicated Woman/Petite size-range banners; copy is fixed, not written fresh.
- **Lauren Look Banner** — fixed "Introducing THE LAUREN LOOK" rental-subscription promo module; copy is fixed.
- **Complete the Look (CTL)** — when this brand uses one, its title runs at the brand's secondary heading level, one step down from a regular module title (see `module-types.md`).
