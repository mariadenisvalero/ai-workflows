# RRL (Double RL) — brand reference (copy voice only)

Not one of the four brands `figma-workflow.md` and the other brand files cover for template-building — this brand isn't in the "EMAIL TEMPLATES" library scope this skill was built against. Included here in case copy work or a future template build touches it. Source: RL Master Tone & Voice Guidelines.

## Overview
RRL is Ralph Lauren's specialty brand for men and women — rugged, well-worn looks inspired by the American West, workwear, and utilitarian pieces originally built for practical purposes (military gear, farm-team uniforms). A brand of few words: practical, to the point, down to earth, naturally elegant with a light old-timey touch. Focus on craftsmanship and quality alongside vintage inspiration.

## The story
Founded 1993, named for Mr. Lauren's Colorado ranch (Double RL). *"The cowboy lives a certain kind of life. They're tough and there's a certain look to the clothes they wear... These clothes grew out of an old tradition. They are part of the land."* — Ralph Lauren. Draws on the West, Midwest, and Southwest, regional craftsmanship, and American heritage.

## Tone pillars
- **HISTORICAL** — steeped in Americana across many subcultures/eras (cattle ranches, vintage shops, hot-rod garages, military surplus, turn-of-the-century workwear supply); adapts the period-accurate language of each.
- **AUTHENTIC** — never resorts to camp; grounded in real wording from actual vintage signage and period language.
- **CONCISE** — quick-hitting, detail-focused snapshots (like vintage store signage), not long explanatory sentences, even though the product details themselves are intricate.

## Product copy discipline
Target length: 30–50 words, 1–3 short sentences, free of superlatives. Key words: Vintage-Inspired, Utilitarian, Rugged, Workwear, Quality. See `pdp-product-copy.md` for the full cross-brand bullet/romance-copy structure.
