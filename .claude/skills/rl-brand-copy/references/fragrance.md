# Fragrance — brand reference (copy voice only)

Not one of the four brands `figma-workflow.md` and the other brand files cover for template-building. Included here in case fragrance copy or a future template touches it. Source: RL Master Tone & Voice Guidelines.

## Overview
Ralph Lauren fragrances tell an aromatic story tied to the lifestyles of the RL world — capturing allure, sophistication, mystery, masculinity, seduction, speed, and more. Built on Mr. Lauren's belief in scents that grow with the wearer: experiences, moments, aspirations, memories.

## The story
Each fragrance transports the reader somewhere specific — an English manor house, a Jamaican isle, a Kentucky polo field, a New York Art Deco nightclub — capturing the scene and story that inspired it.

## Tone pillars
- **ELEVATED** — luxury without condescension; noble, refined, dignified, yet simple and clear.
- **EVOCATIVE** — exacting, precise adjectives that build a textural collage of feeling; never verbose.
- **ROMANTIC** — "romantic" here means dream-like and escapist, *not* love-affair romance. Idealizes the inspiration behind the scent. Avoid magical/fanciful/ethereal wording even though the mood is dreamy.

## Copyediting note
**Capitalize fragrance notes** (e.g. Black Vanilla, Tobacco) — they're treated as distinct, named ingredients, not generic nouns.

## Approved vs. incorrect examples
- Approved product description: *"Fragrant Jasmine. Sweet Rose. Delicate blooms. The essence of love comes alive with the #RalphLaurenRomance collection of fragrances."*
- Incorrect (too generic/off-tone): *"Fuel your adrenaline this season with #PoloRed Eau de Parfum."* — this reads as a seasonal-trend hook, which fights the timeless, dream-like register fragrance copy is supposed to hold.
