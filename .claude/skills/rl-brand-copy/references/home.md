# Home — brand voice

For typography, color hex codes, type scale, padding, and fixed-module visual specs, see the Figma assembly skill instead — this file is copy/story only.

## Logo
Home has one logo, not several sub-lines — the plain "RALPH LAUREN" wordmark. No Woman/Petite/Men's-style split to decide between.

## Overview
Introduced 1983. A leading arbiter of artisanal luxury and one of the world's premier home-furnishings brands — furniture, lighting, floorcovering, fabric/wallcovering, china/crystal/silver, decorative accessories, bed and bath linens. Synonymous with enduring style and the highest level of craftsmanship. Home inherits the Master Brand pillars (`master-brand.md`).

## The story
"Whether we live in the city, the country, on a farm, at the beach, or in a penthouse, each is home, and just as we dress differently in each of these places, we want to live differently as well." — Ralph Lauren. Home introduced the idea of "dressing a bed" the same way Mr. Lauren layers a runway look.

## Tone pillars
- **REFINED** — articulate, eloquent, endearing; never too much or too little.
- **CHARMING** — playful storytelling themes; but, as with Lauren, never whimsical, poetic, or winky.
- **INVITING** — warm, welcoming, comfortable; themes of togetherness, family, relaxation, joy.

## Aesthetic buckets
Home's collections are organized into four moods; use the keyword bank to calibrate copy (and, when relevant, which images from the mapping fit) to the right one rather than defaulting to generic "home" language:
- **Estate** — European country-house grandeur: manor house, equestrian/saddlery, heritage plaids, mahogany, tartan, Georgian/Regency, quiet wealth, heirloom.
- **Cottage & Country** — seaside-to-mountain-retreat ease: romantic, coastal, western, cozy, Americana, bohemian, ticking stripes, chambray, patchwork quilts, flea-market/collected feel.
- **Penthouse** — Old Hollywood/Art Deco confidence: sleek architectural lines, black lacquer, evening-wear-inspired details, cinematic, graphic elegance.
- **Modern Casual** — pared-down, contemporary: minimalist, streamlined, industrial utility, eclectic accents blended with traditional pieces, "curated yet casual, elegant yet effortless."

## Story best practices
Consistent look and feel across a campaign; avoid repeating identical image crops within one email; mix full-outfit/still-life shots with closer crops; add laydowns/styled-detail shots where the image mapping offers them.
