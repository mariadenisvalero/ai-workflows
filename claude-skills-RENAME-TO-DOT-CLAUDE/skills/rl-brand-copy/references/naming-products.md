# How to name products

Source: TAG's RL Copy Master Guide (How to Name Products tab).

## Generic PDP names
Follow this formula (or a variation of it depending on product type):
- **Apparel**: Fit (e.g. "Classic Fit") + Fabrication or Pattern + Product Type (e.g. "Polo Shirt")
- **Accessories**: Fabrication + Size + Formal Name + Bag Type (e.g. "Bag / Tote / Crossbody")

This naming happens as part of writing the rest of the product copy — typically 8–2 weeks before start-ship.

## Proprietary names
Names like "The Ricky Bag" or "The Whitman Chino" are created by the Brand Merch team (not Product Copy), in partnership with legal, global marketing, and D&I. These are usually finalized during market/adoption — at least 6 months before start-ship — and enter the system as base data before Product Copy ever touches the item. If a proprietary name is missing or seems wrong, that's a Brand Merch question, not something to invent.
