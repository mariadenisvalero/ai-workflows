# Copy voice — RLE (Ralph Lauren E-commerce emails)

Source: TAG's RL Copy Master Guide_2025. This covers copy voice and structure; for the build-side (typography, colors, module anatomy) see the per-brand files and `figma-workflow.md`. A fuller brand-by-brand tone breakdown lives in RL's own "Final Style Guide" (external, not in this skill yet — ask the user for it if a brand's specific voice matters and isn't covered below).

## Overall tone (all RLE)
Simple, clear, concise — yet refined and romantic. Not overly verbose, not trying to impress. **The content of the story matters more than the flourishes used to describe the scene or setting.** Content > decoration, in copy the same way it applies to visual design.

## Brand list (copy side — slightly finer-grained than the design brand guide)
Women's Polo, Men's Polo, Kids Polo, Baby, Lauren Ralph Lauren, RLX, Home, Purple Label, Collection, World Of. (The design brand guide groups these under four umbrellas: Polo, Lauren, Home, Luxe Brands — see `polo.md` / `lauren.md` / `home.md` / `luxe-brands.md`.)

## Women's Polo voice (the one brand with detailed guidance here)
Recently shifted to speak directly to a dynamic, self-assured, culturally aware woman:
- Clear, precise, occasion-driven — explain **why** a piece was designed, **how** to style it, **why** it belongs in her wardrobe.
- Balance sophistication with approachability — reads like advice from a trusted friend or sisterhood, never a sales pitch.
- Celebrates her busy, fulfilled life; she is shopping for herself, for her own needs.
- Tone reference points: Staud, J.Crew, Goop, Olivia Palermo — modern and lifestyle-driven, while keeping Polo's own signature polish.
- Styling tips should feel intentional, empowering, and relevant to daily life — not generic.

## Anatomy of an RLE email
```
[Subject]
  SL   — subject line
  PH   — preheader

[Hero]
  Hed  — headline
  Dek  — body/deck copy
  CTA

[Module 1]
  Product name, or a "romance" header
  Optional dek
  CTA

[Module 2]
  (repeat pattern per module)
```

## Worked examples (verbatim from the guide — good calibration for tone/length)

**W Polo — Relaxed Pants**
- SL: Relaxed Pants
- PH: Sophisticated Silhouettes That Embrace Comfort
- Hed: Loosen Up
- Dek: Barrel silhouettes, wide legs, and front pleats: The season's pants are in full-on relaxation mode
- CTAs: SHOP ALL PANTS / SHOP THE RICKY PANT / SHOP DENIM PANTS / SHOP THE GRAPHIC CARGO PANT

**Lauren Ralph Lauren — Dress Shop**
- SL: New In: The Dress Shop
- PH: From Denim Shirtdresses to Sequined Gowns
- Hed: Dress Shop
- Dek: Timeless styles re-imagined with western influences and feminine sophistication
- Module CTA: THE DRESS SHOP
- Module — Daytime Casual: Cool denim, metallic hues, and more for effortless daytime dressing → SHOP CASUAL DRESSES
- Module — Evening Elegance: Make a statement in sequined embroideries and cascading fringe → SHOP FORMAL DRESSES
- [Woman logo] Explore new dresses in sizes 14–22 → SHOP NOW

**Purple Label — Accessories**
- SL: Accessories of Lasting Style
- PH: Perfect Complements to a Considered Wardrobe
- Hed: ACCESSORIES, PERFECTED
- Dek: Timeless signatures and seasonal styles, made to the highest standards of luxury craftsmanship
- CTA: SHOP NOW
- Module — Watches & Eyewear: An elegant pair of sunglasses or a finely crafted watch adds a polished sophistication → SHOP WATCHES / SHOP SUNGLASSES
- Module — Bags: Crafted with exceptionally supple and luxurious leathers, finished with antiquated-brass hardware and hand-stitched details → SHOP BAGS
- Module — Shoes: From Italian-crafted dress shoes to elevated espadrilles → SHOP SHOES

Note the register shift for Purple Label vs. W Polo: shorter, more clipped, material/craft-focused ("antiquated-brass hardware and hand-stitched details") — matches Luxe Brands' more restrained, luxury-code voice vs. Polo's friendlier, more conversational one.

## Performance / social copy (separate from RLE emails)
Two types:
1. **Conversion** — success = purchase. Copy should push "shop now." Usually includes the shipping line: *"Free shipping with an RL account & free returns."*
2. **Consideration** — success = clicks. More lifestyle-focused, intriguing, encourages browsing. Typically **no** shipping line.

**Character limits** (as indicated in the brief, but typical): headline ~27 characters; romance/body copy 50–150 characters. Shipping copy, when included, counts toward that limit.

**Do not include shipping copy** for luxury brands (Purple Label & Collection) or Ralph Lauren Stores (RLS) — even for Conversion copy.

Examples:
- Kids Back to School — Conversion: "Styles They'll Fall For" / "Shop cozy quilting, playful plaids, and more. Free shipping with an RL account & free returns." — Consideration: "Fall Layers" / "Heritage-inspired motifs and warm knitwear take them from school days to weekend outings."
- M Polo Hamptons — Conversion: "In the Hamptons" / "Shop the rustic elegance of life on the coast. Free shipping with an RL account & free returns." — Consideration: "A Seaside Retreat" / "Shop the sophisticated ease of the Hamptons, a vision of coastal elegance and timeless American style."

## PRO (Polo Retail Outlets) — a different, more casual register
PRO is a direct descendant of Polo, for the whole family (men/women/kids — "family" is a running theme), driving **in-store** traffic (PRO has no on-site product pages; it's a landing page with Store Locator info). Tagline: *"To live the American Dream in Spirited Style."* Tone is more casual/accessible than mainline RLE, no luxury or special-collection register to worry about.

## World Of / RLX / Baby
Listed as brands under the copy program but not given their own tone section in this guide — treat cautiously: don't assume they inherit Polo's or Lauren's voice wholesale, ask if it matters for the task at hand.
