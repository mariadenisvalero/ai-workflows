# Ralph Lauren quotes — sourced, first-person

Source: TAG's RL Copy Master Guide (Quotes tab, "Approved by Cybil & Carter"). Per the narrator rule in `master-brand.md`, **quotes are the one place first-person copy is allowed** — but only genuine, sourced quotes, never invented ones. This file is a curated subset (the source has ~150); it's organized by theme the way the source is, so pick the quote that fits the story rather than the first one that scans well. If nothing here fits, say so rather than fabricating a quote in Ralph Lauren's voice.

## From the 50th Anniversary Book (thematic, undated within the book)

**Inspiration**
- "I am constantly drawing inspiration from the places I travel, the people I meet, the movies I see, the heroes I admire."
- "To design without fashion in mind, to design something legendary that has a sense of timelessness, a foreverness that still excites us, is what I aspire to."
- "The world is open to us, and each day is an occasion to reinvent ourselves."

**Style & living**
- "Style is very personal. It has nothing to do with fashion. Fashion is over very quickly. Style is forever."
- "Personal style is about having a sense of yourself and of what you believe in, which is basically self-confidence."
- "For me, luxury is a sensibility, an approach to life. It's not about the season's newest anything. It's about personal style and creating an environment of comfort and ease."
- "Classic to me means things that are timeless and enduring, the things that never go out of style."

**Quality**
- "I don't design for obsolescence — I've designed for longevity."
- "Luxury is quality and timeless elegance."

**Family**
- "Everything I've done has come out of my own living as a man, as a husband, as a father and a friend. I think we all want to make a good life for ourselves and our loved ones, to be happy and free to express who we are."
- "Home is where the heart is, and that is where we seek quality and comfort and a style that is about the people and the family that create it, that live it."

**Pride**
- "Be anything you want to be. And be many things."
- "The bravest choices you can make are to be yourself and remain true to what you love."

## From the Library (press-sourced, individually cited)
- "Why the name Polo? Well, what kind of people play polo? Wealthy, cosmopolitan, chic… I wanted to create a concept for the name." — Collins, Nancy, *WWD*, October 1974
- "I'm like a writer. I express a point of view and a dream to lots of people. I express a story to them in clothes." — Breul, David, *Avenue*, November 1976
- "I am not just making a dress, I am writing a story." *(recurring line, 50th Anniversary Book)*
- "I don't try to create images or products but, rather, worlds where everything is connected — the clothes people wear, the homes they live in, the cars they drive, and the lives they lead." — Aquilina-Ross, Geoffrey, *FHM*, October 1991
- "Other designers do clothes. I do life." — Pittel, Christine, *Elle Décor*, April 1993
- "I have always admired people who were timeless. No age or period to them... Nothing I make could ever be labelled 'last year's look.'" — Cavendish, Lucy, *Sunday Telegraph*, 5/9/99
- "American luxury is about a sensibility and an approach to life. It's about developing the world you see in your imagination and using that to create an environment of comfort and ease." — Trebay, Guy, *The New York Times Style Magazine*, 8/26/07

## Usage note
Full citation format matters if a quote is used in published copy — keep the attribution (publication + date) with it rather than presenting it as an unsourced line. If a campaign needs a quote on a theme not represented here (e.g. sport, travel, a specific decade), ask the user to pull it from the full source document rather than approximating one.
