# RL Glossary — abbreviations

Source: TAG's RL Glossary. Consult this whenever a brief, Figma comment, or copy doc uses an unfamiliar short code.

## Ralph Lauren fiscal year
- Q1 — April / May / June
- Q2 — July / August / September
- Q3 — October / November / December
- Q4 — January / February / March

## Brands
- **RL** — Ralph Lauren
- **PL** — Purple Label
- **RLH** — Ralph Lauren Home
- **RL VE** — Ralph Lauren Virtual Experience
- **PFS** — Polo Factory Store
- **LRL** — Lauren Ralph Lauren

## Workstreams
- **BM** — Brand Media
- **PFS** — Polo Factory Store
- **PRO** — Polo Retail Outlets (the newer internal name for PFS as a workstream)
- **RLE** — Ralph Lauren E-commerce
- **RLS** — Ralph Lauren Store

## Email sections / building blocks
- **CTL** — Complete the Look
- **WIW** — Wear it With
- **CYO** — Create Your Own
- **HED** — Header copy
- **DEK** — Body copy
- **MI** — Movable Ink
- **MWK** — Men, Women and Kids
- **FOB** — Family of Business (a picture featuring Men/Women/Kids together)
- **FPO** — For Placement Only (non-retouched images)
- **LLB** — Lauren Look Banner
- **CTA** — Call to Action
- **BAU** — Business As Usual
- **OLA** — Online Advertising
- **POS** — Point of Sale
- **SF** — Salesforce
- **DTS** — Drive to Store
- **OOH** — Out of Home

## PFS-specific
- **MMS** — Multimedia Messaging Service
- **TAA** — Take an Additional (an extra percentage of discount stacked on top of an existing offer)

## General office shorthand
- **TBD** — To Be Decided
- **ETA** — Estimated Time of Arrival
- **TK** — To Come
- **EOD** — End of Day
- **EOW** — End of Week
- **FYI** — For Your Information
- **BTW** — By The Way
- **AT** — Airtable
- **CODE Team (PFS)** — owns campaign metrics/performance; uses that data to decide when to increase advertising, start promo sales, etc.
